Eight Powerful Modules Built for Indian Retail

High-Speed POS Billing Terminal

Generate itemized invoices in under 5 seconds. Includes dynamic live UPI QR code generation so customers can scan with PhonePe/GPay/Paytm, automatic cash change return calculator, and custom discount controls.

Barcode Scanner & MRP Vision

Scan product barcodes with mobile camera or USB handheld scanner guns. Auto-identifies product, MRP, selling price, and stock levels instantly.

Digital Customer Udhaar Khata
