Complete SaaS Suite

Eight Powerful Modules in One Cohesive OS

Every tool your retail store needs to operate efficiently from day one.

📦

Inventory Management

Track stock quantities in real time, monitor wholesale cost prices vs retail selling margins, and receive automatic alerts when items drop below minimum safety stock.

👥

Customer CRM & Loyalty

Maintain organized customer profiles with complete purchase history, phone numbers, and automatic loyalty reward tiers (Bronze, Silver, Gold).

📒

Udhaar (Credit) Ledger

Digital credit book. Know exactly who owes you money and send instant courteous WhatsApp payment reminders with your shop UPI ID attached.

🧾

Digital & Thermal Invoices

Generate GST-ready bills in under 5 seconds. Print standard 80mm thermal receipts or send formatted itemized digital invoices directly to WhatsApp.

👔

Staff Directory

Manage helpers, billing cashiers, and delivery staff with contact information, roles, and agreed monthly salary allowances.

📅

Daily Attendance Register

Interactive 31-day monthly attendance grid. One-click status cycling between Present (Green), Half-Day (Yellow), and Absent (Red) with automatic payroll estimates.

📈

Financial Analytics & P&L

Visual SVG revenue trend charts, categorized store expense tracking (rent, bills, logistics), and net in-pocket profit calculations.

✨

MyDukaan AI Advisor

Conversational business consultant powered by Google Gemini 3.8 Flash. Analyzes your live store data to provide actionable inventory, margin, and debt recovery advice.
