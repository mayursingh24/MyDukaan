Frequently Asked Questions

Everything you need to know about using MyDukkan24 in your store.

Kya MyDukaan24 internet ke bina (offline) chalta hai? +
Haan, bilkul! MyDukaan24 100% offline-first architecture par bana hai. Saare products, billing records, customers aur khata aapke device ke local database me securely store hote hain. Internet na hone par bhi aap fast billing, barcode scan aur receipts nikaal sakte hain.
Kya isme supermarket jaisi USB barcode gun connect kar sakte hain? +
Haan! Aap computer/laptop se koi bhi standard USB barcode scanner gun connect kar sakte hain. Saath hi aap apne Android phone ya tablet ke camera ko bhi high-speed barcode vision scanner ke roop me use kar sakte hain.
GST aur Non-GST dono tarah ke bills ban sakte hain? +
Haan, aap Settings me apna GSTIN number aur default tax percentage (0%, 5%, 12%, 18%) set kar sakte hain. Agar aap non-GST small retailer hain, toh 0% tax choose karke clean simple receipt bana sakte hain.
Mera store data kahan save hota hai? Kya yeh offline aur online dono me secure hai? +
Aapka data dual-layer storage me rehta hai: aapke device par 0ms Offline LocalStorage + IndexedDB cache me aur Firebase login ke baad aapke private Cloud Firestore account (/users/{uid}/) me real-time sync hota hai. Internet na ho tab bhi billing rukti nahi, aur online aate hi sab auto-sync ho jata hai.
Kya iske liye koi monthly subscription fee deni hogi? +
Nahi! MyDukaan24 100% free software platform hai. Isme koi hidden monthly subscription ya per-bill charge nahi hai. Aap ise zindagi bhar muft me use kar sakte hain.
